## Why

Issue [#1819](https://github.com/Kulitorum/Decenza/issues/1819): the DE1 reported itself connected while three of its five notification streams had never been enabled. `STATE_INFO`, `SHOT_SAMPLE` and `WATER_LEVELS` each failed their CCCD write with `DescriptorWriteError` and then timed out. The app charted nothing, detected no shot, and never stopped at weight; the reporter had to stop each shot by hand and downgrade to get a usable machine.

The cause is that two peripherals had GATT operations outstanding at the same time. Qt serializes GATT work **per `QLowEnergyController`** — `readWriteQueue` and `pendingJob` are instance fields, not statics ([`QtBluetoothLE.java:949-950`](https://github.com/qt/qtconnectivity/blob/6.11.1/src/android/bluetooth/src/org/qtproject/qt/android/bluetooth/QtBluetoothLE.java)) — so nothing in the framework prevents a second peripheral's service discovery from landing on top of the DE1's descriptor writes. In the captured session the scale's connect and characteristic discovery ran from 23.0 s to 37.9 s, straight across the DE1's CCCD writes at 29.2 s, 32.3 s and 35.3 s.

de1app makes this state unreachable, and not by handling the error better: it puts DE1 *and* scale commands into one queue (`::de1(cmdstack)`, single `::de1(wrote)` in-flight flag) so two GATT operations cannot be outstanding at once, and it defers a scale connect while that shared queue is more than two deep (`bluetooth.tcl:2276`, "Too much backpressure, waiting with the connect").

decaid is a **counter-example on the queue and a precedent on the failure handling**. It sets `UniversalBle.queueType = QueueType.perDevice` (`universal_ble_discovery_service.dart:403`) — serialized per device, deliberately not across devices — so it does not prevent this interleaving at all. But on a failed subscribe it throws out of `_bleConnect()`, so the whole connect attempt fails and the reconnect ladder re-runs it against a fresh GATT. Decenza was alone in proceeding to report `CONNECTED` on a link whose telemetry never started.

Our existing mitigation is a 15 s timer that releases the scale's connect if the DE1 has not finished ([`blemanager.cpp:113`](../../../src/ble/blemanager.cpp)). It expired in both failing sessions and is a timer used as a guard, which the project forbids. It also covers only one of the orderings: refractometers share `ScaleBleTransport` with the scales and connect **on demand when the user opens the review page**, so an R2 can start a GATT connect mid-shot with nothing gating it at all.

## What Changes

- **One GATT queue for every peripheral.** A single queue holds every GATT operation — connect, service discovery, characteristic/descriptor read or write — from the DE1, every scale and every refractometer, with one operation dispatched at a time. This is de1app's `::de1(cmdstack)` and its single `::de1(wrote)` flag. `BleTransport::m_commandQueue` is deleted rather than gated: two queues would leave ordering decided in more than one place.
- **The scale and refractometer transports gain in-flight tracking they do not have today.** `QtScaleBleTransport` and `CoreBluetoothScaleBleTransport` currently issue every operation straight to the platform with no completion matching, no retry and no depth accounting — which is why they cannot yield to the DE1. Queueing gives them an in-flight concept for the first time.
- **Per-operation policy, not per-queue.** The DE1's tuned retry budget, urgent-write path and UUID-scoped discard travel with its operations and must come through unchanged. Scale and refractometer operations default to **zero retries**, preserving today's fire-and-forget semantics exactly: inheriting the DE1's 5-retry budget would let a dead scale link hold the shared slot through tens of seconds of retries and starve the machine the budget exists to protect.
- **Unconditional, one code path on every platform.** The per-controller queue is backend-independent, so the defect is not platform-specific; a single Android log is where it was observed, not the limit of where it can happen. No `#ifdef`, no platform-gated behaviour.
- **No new timers, and one deleted.** Dispatch of the next operation is driven by the terminal outcome of the current one — its success callback or its error callback. `SUBSCRIBE_TIMEOUT_MS` is removed: it duplicates Qt's own per-operation 3 s bound (`RUNNABLE_TIMEOUT`, `QtBluetoothLE.java:69`) and races it, which is what turned a 45 ms `DescriptorWriteError` into a 3 s stall three times over in the captured session.
- **A failed notification subscribe fails the connect.** `DescriptorWriteError` during `subscribeAll()` currently logs at DEBUG and is dropped, and the 3 s timeout then proceeds to the next stream. The error already arrives — at +45 ms in the captured session — so handling it needs no clock. CCCD writes go through the shared queue like every other operation, inheriting its bounded retry, and on exhaustion the connect fails and the existing reconnect ladder re-runs subscription against a fresh GATT. **BREAKING** for the current behaviour where a link with dead telemetry still emits `connected()` and reports `DE1 CONNECTED (BLE)`.
- **No unbounded in-place CCCD retry.** de1app retries a `vital` command every 500 ms indefinitely with the command pinned at the head of a now-stalled queue; its own source comments that this "kind of kills the BLE abilities of the app". Bounded retry through the shared queue, then fail the connect.
- **Remove the 15 s DE1-wait timer.** Ordering comes from the shared queue, which covers scales and refractometers alike. The debugging case it was written for (no DE1 present) is already an event — discovery failure or no-device-found.
- **Subscribe outcome is logged at the user-visible tier and names its stream.** Today `Descriptor error (suppressed)` is DEBUG, so the connections views never show it; the error line does not name the UUID, so which stream died had to be inferred by counting against `subscribeAll()`'s queue order.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `ble-gatt-serialization`: every GATT operation from every peripheral passes through one queue with a single operation outstanding at a time; ordering, event-driven dispatch with no queue-owned clock, per-operation retry policy, and discard on teardown.

### Modified Capabilities

- `de1-connection-health`: a DE1 link whose required notification subscriptions did not complete is not reported as connected — it is torn down and reconnected. Adds the requirement that each subscription's outcome is observable at the tier the connections views display, naming the stream.
- `device-reconnect`: scale and refractometer connects are ordered behind an in-progress DE1 connect by the shared queue rather than by a 15 s timer, and the same ordering applies to a refractometer connect initiated mid-session.

## Impact

- **`src/ble/` (new shared queue)** — owns the single operation queue and the in-flight slot, dispatches one operation at a time, and carries each operation's own retry and pacing policy. Not a new thread; it dispatches on the existing event loop.
- **`src/ble/bletransport.{h,cpp}`** — `m_commandQueue`, `m_commandTimer` and the local `m_writePending` gate are replaced by submissions to the shared queue. Retry budget, `writeUrgent` and `discardQueued()` semantics come through as per-operation policy. The 50 ms pacing is deliberately NOT carried across — it was armed only on an enqueue that found the link idle, so it delayed the first write after a pause and never paced consecutive writes. `SUBSCRIBE_TIMEOUT_MS` is deleted and CCCD writes are submitted like any other operation. Descriptor-error handling classifies and fails the connect instead of logging at DEBUG and advancing.
- **`src/ble/transport/qtscalebletransport.{h,cpp}` and `src/ble/transport/corebluetooth/corebluetoothscalebletransport.{h,mm}`** — submit operations to the shared queue and match their completion signals, which requires adding in-flight tracking these classes do not currently have. Default policy is zero retries, matching today's behaviour. Covers all 14 scales and both DiFluid refractometers without touching each device class.
- **`src/ble/blemanager.cpp`** — delete `m_de1WaitTimer`, `m_scaleConnectDeferred` and the 15 s branch in `tryDirectConnectToScale()`; ordering is now a property of the shared queue. `setServiceDiscoveryActive()` is subsumed and removed.
- **`src/ble/refractometers/`** — no per-device changes expected; coverage comes from `ScaleBleTransport`. Verified rather than assumed: an R2 connect started from the review page must still complete while the DE1 is connected.
- **`tests/`** — the shared queue's ordering, per-operation policy and teardown discard are unit-testable without a radio, and the DE1's existing retry/pacing/discard behaviour must be pinned before and after the move. A DE1 connect whose CCCD write fails must not emit `connected()`.
- **`docs/CLAUDE_MD/BLE_PROTOCOL.md`** — record the shared queue as the mechanism, and that Qt's queue is per-controller so the app must supply the cross-device half.
- No BLE protocol change, no new setting, no schema migration, no user-visible UI change beyond the DE1 no longer reporting connected when it is not usable.
